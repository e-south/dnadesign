# Geometry Switchboard 20B

This deliverable refreshes the browser-facing atlas control plane.
It exists so operators can compare persisted 7B and 20B pooled spaces without
recomputing ad hoc notebook state.

The switchboard is for navigation and QC.
Use it to inspect the saved projections, confirm the wild-type reference set is
visible across panels, and hand off into the browser notebook.
Do not treat it as the final decision artifact for the study.
