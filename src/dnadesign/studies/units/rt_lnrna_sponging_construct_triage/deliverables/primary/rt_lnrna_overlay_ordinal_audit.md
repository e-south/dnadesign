## RT-lnRNA Overlay Ordinal Audit

Source-scoped Khan and Crawford abundance ordinal checks. Khan and
Crawford values remain separate source axes and are not interpreted as a shared
numeric scale.

### rt_lnrna_overlay_ordinal_audit | Overlay ordinal audit

Summarizes whether source-scoped Khan and Crawford ordinal groups form coherent
ordered axes in each representation view.

### rt_lnrna_abundance_margin_ladder_gallery | Abundance margin ladder

Shows row-level endpoint margins across source-scoped abundance groups.

### rt_lnrna_abundance_margin_scatter_gallery | Abundance margin scatter

Plots abundance assay value against endpoint margin to expose source-specific
row-level ladder behavior.
