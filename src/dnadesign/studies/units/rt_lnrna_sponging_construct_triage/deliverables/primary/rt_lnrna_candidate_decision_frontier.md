## RT-lnRNA Candidate Decision Frontier

Decision surface restricted to the four intermediate candidate-X views. The
frontier balances representation health, source/design-structure preservation,
abundance ordinal signal, context robustness, and dimensional cost.

### rt_lnrna_candidate_decision_frontier | Candidate decision frontier

Synthesizes source-family separation and Crawford abundance ordinal signal over
the four intermediate candidate-X views.

### rt_lnrna_candidate_x_scorecard | Candidate-X scorecard

Lists health, source-structure, abundance, context, and dimensional-cost
metrics so candidate-X tradeoffs are visible without defaulting to the largest
concat.
